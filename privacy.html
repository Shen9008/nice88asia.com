Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026. This policy explains what personal data Nice88 Asia collects, how we use it, and your rights as a data subject.

1. Who We Are

Nice88 Asia operates the online casino and sportsbook at nice88asia.com. We hold a gaming licence issued by the Government of Curaçao (Licence No. 8048/JAZ2017-067). For data-related enquiries, contact us at support@nice88asia.com.

2. Data We Collect

We collect data that is necessary to operate a licensed gambling service:

  • Registration data — name, date of birth, email address, country of residence, and username
  • Identity verification data — government-issued ID document, proof of address, and (where required) source of funds documentation. This is a regulatory requirement under our Curaçao licence and AML obligations.
  • Financial data — deposit and withdrawal history, payment method details (processed under PCI-DSS compliant systems; full card numbers are not stored by us)
  • Gameplay data — game session records, bet history, and bonus usage for regulatory audit and responsible gambling monitoring
  • Communication data — emails, live chat transcripts, and support tickets
  • Technical data — IP address, device type, browser, and session timestamps, used for security and fraud detection

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate your account and process transactions
  • To comply with our licence obligations — age verification, AML checks, responsible gambling monitoring
  • To protect against fraud, money laundering, and unauthorised account access
  • To communicate with you about your account, promotions you have opted into, and regulatory notices
  • To improve the platform based on aggregated, anonymised usage analytics

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Sharing

We share personal data only where necessary:

  • Game providers — providers such as Pragmatic Play and Evolution Gaming may receive session data as required for game operation and their own regulatory compliance
  • Payment processors — transaction data is shared with the payment provider you choose, under their own privacy terms
  • Regulatory authorities — we may be required to provide account data to the Government of Curaçao or law enforcement agencies under applicable law
  • Fraud prevention services — aggregated risk signals may be shared with third-party fraud detection systems

5. Data Security

  • All connections use 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption
  • Passwords are stored as one-way cryptographic hashes — no staff member can read your password
  • Payment processing complies with PCI-DSS standards
  • Two-factor authentication is available for additional account security
  • Access to personal data is restricted to staff with a legitimate operational need

6. Data Retention

We retain account data for a minimum of five years after account closure, as required by our AML and regulatory obligations. Gameplay and transaction records are retained for the same period. You may request deletion of data that is not subject to a retention requirement by contacting support@nice88asia.com.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to correction — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of data not subject to retention requirements
  • Right to restrict processing — request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances
  • Right to data portability — request your data in a structured, machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@nice88asia.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to: maintain your session, remember your preferences, detect fraud, and collect aggregated analytics. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect functionality.

9. Changes to This Policy

We will notify registered users of material changes to this policy via email. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
