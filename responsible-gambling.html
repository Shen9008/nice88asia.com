Responsible Gambling

Gambling at Nice88 Asia should always be entertainment — never a source of income or financial pressure. This page explains the tools available to help you stay in control, and where to find free, confidential support if gambling is no longer fun.

18+
Nice88 Asia is strictly for adults aged 18 and over. Age is verified at registration and at first withdrawal. If you are under 18, you may not create an account or access any gambling product on this site.

Account Controls — Available Right Now

Every Nice88 Asia account includes built-in tools to limit or pause your play. You can activate any of these from Account Settings > Responsible Gambling after logging in, or by contacting our 24/7 support team.

Deposit Limits

Set a maximum amount you can deposit over a daily, weekly, or monthly period. Once your limit is reached, further deposits are blocked until the period resets. Decreasing a limit takes effect immediately. Increasing a limit requires a 24-hour cooling-off period before it activates.

Loss Limits

Set a maximum net loss you're willing to accept in a day, week, or month. When your net losses reach that threshold, your account is automatically restricted from further wagers for the remainder of the period.

Session Time Reminders

Set a duration after which you receive an on-screen notification reminding you how long you've been playing. The reminder doesn't force a stop — it gives you a conscious moment to decide whether to continue.

Cool-Off Period

Temporarily suspend your account for a period between 24 hours and 6 weeks. During a cool-off, you cannot log in, deposit, or place any bets. Your account balance is preserved and withdrawals remain available. Cool-off is activated immediately and cannot be lifted early.

Self-Exclusion

Permanently close your account. Self-exclusion requests are processed immediately — there is no cooling-off period, no delay, and no persuasion from our team. Once active, your account cannot be reopened. Any remaining balance will be returned to you via your usual withdrawal method. To self-exclude, contact support directly or use the account settings panel.

If you are in crisis or in immediate financial distress, please reach out to a support organisation below — they respond faster than we can.

Warning Signs to Watch For

Gambling can become harmful before you notice it. These are common warning signs:

  • Spending more than you planned, or chasing losses by betting more
  • Gambling with money set aside for rent, bills, food, or family needs
  • Lying to people close to you about how much you gamble or spend
  • Feeling anxious, irritable, or depressed when not gambling
  • Using gambling as an escape from problems or low mood
  • Borrowing money or selling possessions to fund gambling
  • Missing work, study, or family commitments because of gambling

If any of these apply, consider using the cool-off or self-exclusion tool above, or contact one of the support organisations below.

Free & Confidential Support Organisations

These organisations provide free support and do not require you to be in crisis to use them:

BeGambleAware Free advice, self-assessment tools, and a helpline. Available 24/7 via web chat and phone (UK: 0808 8020 133). GamCare Helpline, live chat, counselling, and peer support for players and their families. Gambling Therapy International support in multiple languages including English, Spanish, French, and Portuguese. Online forum and one-to-one support. NCPG (National Council on Problem Gambling) US-based but globally accessible resources including a 24/7 crisis helpline: 1-800-522-4700. Gamblers Anonymous Peer support meetings and a 12-step recovery programme. Local meetings in many countries; online meetings also available.

Parental Controls — Protecting Minors

If you share a device with anyone under 18, we recommend installing parental control software to restrict access to gambling websites. Options include:

  • GamBlock — blocks gambling sites on your device
  • Net Nanny — parental filtering with gambling category blocking
  • BetFilter — gambling-specific site blocking software

Nice88 Asia enforces age verification at both registration and first withdrawal. However, technical controls on shared devices are an additional layer of protection we encourage all households to use.
